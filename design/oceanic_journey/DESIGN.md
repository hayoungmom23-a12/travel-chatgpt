## Brand & Style

The brand personality is that of an effortless, clairvoyant travel companion—refreshing, airy, and brimming with wanderlust. It eliminates the cognitive overload of trip planning through calm, structured intelligence paired with moments of sunlit delight. The UI evokes the sensation of looking out over an open ocean horizon: infinite possibility, absolute clarity, and invigorating momentum.

The target audience spans modern digital travelers, nomadic professionals, and deliberate explorers who value both curated recommendations and frictionless autonomy.

The aesthetic fuses **Modern Contemporary** with subtle **Airy Glassmorphism**:
- Expansive, luminous off-white surfaces that give itinerary cards room to breathe.
- Vibrant oceanic azure anchors the interface with credibility and technical poise.
- Sun-drenched coral highlights inspire spontaneity and direct attention to actionable next steps.
- Hyper-clean typography balances Latin and Hangul glyphs seamlessly, ensuring precision across timelines, route maps, and AI conversational prompts.

## Colors

The color system centers around high-clarity nautical tones counterbalanced by energetic, warm accents:

- **Primary (`#0077B6`)**: Deep sea azure used for primary navigational targets, key interactive milestones, and active AI conversation states.
- **Secondary (`#FF6B4A`)**: Sunkissed coral reserved for high-intent actions (e.g., "Book Flight", "Generate Itinerary"), highlights, promotional indicators, and delightful discovery badges.
- **Tertiary (`#0284C7`)**: Clear sky cyan applied to secondary data chips, active filters, and subtle ambient highlights.
- **Neutrals**:
  - `Surface Base`: `#F8FAFC` (Slate 50) creates an airy, low-fatigue backdrop.
  - `Surface Elevated`: `#FFFFFF` ensures crisp card definition.
  - `Border / Divider`: `#E2E8F0` (Slate 200) and `#CBD5E1` (Slate 300) provide structural definition without visual weight.
  - `Text Primary`: `#0F172A` (Slate 900) delivers high-contrast legibility.
  - `Text Secondary`: `#334155` (Slate 700) handles explanatory text and secondary metadata.
  - `Text Muted`: `#64748B` (Slate 500) grounds timestamps, travel tags, and minor details.

## Typography

The type stack combines the friendly, geometric energy of Plus Jakarta Sans for displays and section headers with the utilitarian clarity of Inter and Pretendard for content, schedules, and Korean localization.

- **Multilingual Harmony**: The fallback order strictly prioritizes `Pretendard` directly behind the primary Latin face to deliver optical weight parity between Latin alphabet characters and Hangul glyphs.
- **Hierarchy Rules**: Numerical data (times, pricing, flight codes) use `Inter` or `Plus Jakarta Sans` with proportional tabular figures enabled (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`).
- **Reading Comfort**: Travel recommendations and conversational response bubbles use `body-lg` or `body-md` with relaxed line heights (1.55 to 1.625) to avoid clutter in dense itineraries.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is anchored on an 8pt spatial grid with generous horizontal margins to preserve an airy, unhurried mindset.

- **Desktop (1200px+)**: 12-column layout with `margin-lg` (3.5rem) and `gutter-lg` (2rem). Supports a split-screen paradigm: AI prompt/chat workspace on the left (5 columns) and an interactive itinerary/map view on the right (7 columns).
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px)**: 8-column layout with `margin` (2rem) and `gutter` (1.5rem). The timeline and map seamlessly transition to a stacked tabbed interface.
- **Mobile (< 768px)**: 4-column layout with `margin-sm` (1rem) and `gutter-sm` (1rem). Floating actions and docked bottom sheets maximize screen real estate for route exploration.
- **Internal Component Spacing**:
  - Tight groupings (badge icons, tags): `space-xs` (4px) to `space-sm` (8px).
  - Input field interiors & compact list cards: `space-md` (16px).
  - Itinerary cards & chat bubbles: `space-lg` (24px).
  - Major section gaps: `space-xl` (40px).

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses subtle surface layering and oceanic tinting rather than heavy, opaque shadows:

- **Surface Levels**:
  - `Level 0 (Canvas)`: `#F8FAFC` flat base canvas.
  - `Level 1 (Cards & Modules)`: Pure `#FFFFFF` resting on Level 0, bound with a delicate border of `rgba(226, 232, 240, 0.8)`. Shadow: `0 2px 8px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04), 0 1px 4px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.02)`.
  - `Level 2 (Hover States & Itinerary Nodes)`: Elevated interactive cards. Shadow: `0 10px 25px -5px rgba(0, 119, 182, 0.08), 0 4px 10px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.03)`.
  - `Level 3 (Sticky Nav, Floating Search & Bottom Sheets)`: Frosted glass finish using `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.85)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)` and shadow: `0 20px 30px -10px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08)`.
  - `Level 4 (Modals & Travel Overlays)`: High-elevation focus surfaces with shadow: `0 25px 50px -12px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.16)`.

## Shapes

The design uses a rounded geometry level of `2` (0.5rem base radius) to convey softness, safety, and modern approachability.

- **Base Radius (`0.5rem` / 8px)**: Used for input fields, action chips, dropdown menus, and internal thumbnail imagery.
- **Large Radius (`1rem` / 16px)**: Applied to itinerary cards, chat bubbles, timeline segments, and map overlays.
- **Extra Large Radius (`1.5rem` / 24px)**: Dedicated to top-level modal sheets, bottom drawers, and persistent floating toolbars.
- **Pill Radius (`9999px`)**: Reserved strictly for action buttons, status pills, filter tags, and agent avatar enclosures.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary**: Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), solid `#0077B6` with white text. Hover transitions to `#0284C7` with subtle elevation. Active states compress slightly (`scale(0.98)`).
- **Secondary / Accent**: Solid `#FF6B4A` with white text. Reserved for key conversion hooks like "Finalize Booking" or "Add to Itinerary".
- **Ghost / Outlined**: Transparent background with 1.5px border in `#E2E8F0` and `#0F172A` text. On hover, background shifts to `rgba(0, 119, 182, 0.05)` with primary border accent.

### Chips & Filter Pills
- Rendered in pill form with 12px horizontal padding and 6px vertical padding.
- Default state: `#FFFFFF` surface with `#E2E8F0` stroke and `#334155` text.
- Selected state: Light oceanic wash `rgba(0, 119, 182, 0.1)` with `#0077B6` text and border. Include travel category icons (e.g., flight, hotel, café) on the leading edge.

### Input Fields & Search Bars
- Background: `#FFFFFF` with `#E2E8F0` 1px border and 12px horizontal padding (`space-md`).
- Focus state: Border transitions to `#0077B6` with an oceanic halo ring `box-shadow: 0 0 0 3px rgba(0, 119, 182, 0.15)`.
- AI Natural Language Prompt Input: Uses an expanded pill shape with an integrated `#FF6B4A` send button and prompt suggestions stacked neatly beneath.

### Cards (Itinerary, Flight & Hotel)
- Pure `#FFFFFF` background with `1rem` corner radius and thin slate border (`#F1F5F9`).
- Padded with `space-lg` (24px). Feature structured sections: time marker on the left, clear typography in the center, and dynamic pricing or booking chips docked on the right.
- Left-side accent indicator: A 3px colored accent bar (`#0077B6` for transit, `#FF6B4A` for highlights, `#10B981` for completed).

### Lists & Timelines
- Connected vertical nodes using a 2px `#E2E8F0` path with 8px circular node markers.
- Active transit legs animate with a subtle oceanic pulse.

### Checkboxes & Radio Buttons
- Checkbox: 18px rounded square (4px radius) in `#0077B6` when checked, with a crisp white check icon.
- Radio: 18px circular ring with an 8px inner dot in `#0077B6`.

### AI Agent Conversational Elements
- **Agent Message**: `#FFFFFF` card with soft glass elevation and a discrete `#0077B6` gradient indicator.
- **User Prompt**: `#0077B6` fill with pure white typography, aligned right with 16px corner radius (bottom-right tucked at 4px).
- **Streaming Response Indicator**: Three oceanic bouncing dots using `animation-delay` sequencing.
